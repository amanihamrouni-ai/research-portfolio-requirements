**Challenge Activity Answer**

Analyzing data from the Square Kilometre Array (SKA) and the James Webb Space Telescope requires several important data skills and technologies.

1. **Big Data Processing:** Distributed computing technologies such as Apache Spark can help process large volumes of astronomical data.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Scientists must detect errors, remove noise, and ensure data quality.
3. **Statistics and Machine Learning:** These techniques can help identify patterns, classify astronomical objects, and detect unusual observations.
4. **Data Visualization:** Charts, images, and interactive visualizations help scientists understand and communicate their discoveries.
5. **Data Storage and Management:** Efficient databases, metadata, backups, and clear documentation are necessary to organize and preserve data over many years.
6. **Automation and Scalability:** Automated pipelines can process new observations continuously and adapt to increasing data volumes.

In the future, scientists will need scalable data infrastructure, reliable processing pipelines, standardized formats, and long-term data preservation strategies. These processes will help researchers analyze astronomical data efficiently and make new scientific discoveries.

Question 1: What types of accounts (products) are available on GitHub?

There are three types of accounts on GitHub. Personal accounts, Organization accounts, and Enterprise accounts.
Question 2: What can you do with a personal account?

Your personal account can own resources such as repositories, packages, and projects. Any time you take any action on GitHub.com, such as creating an issue or reviewing a pull request, the action is attributed to your personal account.
